TessDesk for Windows

A small always-on-top desktop widget that shows what your Tesla's charging is costing you, live, plus battery, tires, rate status and Tesla controls. Data comes from your own Tessie account.

Install

  1. Double-click TessDesk-Setup.exe. It isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC": click More info → Run anyway.
  2. Follow the wizard: I agree → your name → paste your Tessie token, click Check and pick your car → rates (PSO RSEV prefilled) → optional Alexa (Voice Monkey), announcements and reminders → Install.
  3. Setup adds Desktop and Start Menu shortcuts, can start TessDesk with Windows and place it on the screen you choose. Remove it any time from Settings → Apps → TessDesk.

What you need

  • Windows 10 or 11 (PowerShell 5.1 is built in).
  • A Tessie account with your car linked, and its API token (Settings → API).
  • Your electricity rates (or start from the PSO RSEV example preset).

Privacy

  • The download contains no personal data: no token, no settings, no car.
  • Your token stays on your PC (encrypted with Windows DPAPI) and is sent only to api.tessie.com.
