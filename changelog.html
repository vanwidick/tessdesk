Changelog

Every TessDesk version (desktop widget and phone app), newest first.

v4.3.3 Oct 3, 2026

LATEST
  • OPEN TRUNK. A new button opens the rear trunk after an “Are you sure?” pop-up. If the trunk is already open it reads TRUNK OPEN and asks before closing it. There is no frunk button.
  • SENTRY MODE on/off. One button shows the car’s current Sentry state (green outline and text when on) and asks to confirm before turning it on or off.
  • DRIVES. A new card after Tires lists your recent drives: date and time, start → end place, miles, kWh used, how long it took and a cost estimate (the energy used priced at your home off-peak rate). Tap a drive to open its route in Google Maps. Location history lists where the car has been (each place opens on the map) and OPEN MAP shows the recent stops as one route. Read-only, refreshed about every 15 minutes.
  • Paused charging is never counted twice. If charging pauses and resumes on the same plug-in (the car’s energy counter keeps running), it stays one session and only the new kWh are added, however long the pause. If the counter restarted (re-plugged), the earlier part is kept as its own energy, so nothing is lost either.
  • Desktop and phone. Version v4.3.3, new offline cache (tessdesk-v4.3.3). Windows download: TessDesk Setup 4.4.3 (installs widget v4.3.3).

v4.3.2 Oct 3, 2026

  • Last charge = the whole night. Every home charging session from 11 PM to 11 AM the next morning now counts as ONE charge, even if you unplug for 30 minutes and plug back in. The amount, kWh and duration add up across the sessions, and any kWh after 6 AM are priced at the day rate.
  • The whole window glows. Solid red when the car is not plugged in, solid green when it is plugged in (charge complete or limit reached included), and a slow green pulse (about 3.5 s, dimming down and back up) while it is actively charging. The pulse stops the moment charging stops.
  • Flash Lights. New control between Locked and A/C: type how many flashes (1–20, default 3) and tap Flash. It asks first, flashes about 2.5 seconds apart, shows “Flashing 2 of 5” and has a Stop button.
  • Green outlines everywhere. Every control gets the off-peak box’s green outline: Lock, A/C, Heat, Defrost, Overheat, Vent, Close, temperature − and +, Announce on Alexa and each heated seat.
  • Heated seats moved above Tires, and a seat turns solid red while its heat is on (each seat on its own).
  • Desktop and phone. Version v4.3.2, new offline cache (tessdesk-v4.3.2). Windows download: TessDesk Setup 4.4.2 (installs widget v4.3.2).

v4.3.1 Oct 2, 2026

  • Pick your Echos from a list. Announce Setup → Speakers (Echos) now has a dropdown with every speaker on your Voice Monkey account. Tick a speaker to include it in announcements, or keep All Echos on. Each speaker has a ▶ Test button that plays one short line on that Echo only, and only when you click it.
  • + Add speaker. Type a name (for example Kitchen Echo) and tap Open Voice Monkey · Add New Speaker. It opens app.voicemonkey.io/speakers and copies the name. Short steps on screen cover the one step in the Alexa app (a Routine: When this happens → Smart Home → Alexa Voice Monkey v3 → VM Speakers → your speaker, action Voice Monkey, From your Echo). Then Refresh list picks up the new speaker, marked NEW and already ticked. Voice Monkey’s API can list speakers but can’t create them, so the speaker itself is made on the Voice Monkey page.
  • Fixed: the Announce button read “ALL 1 ECHO”. It now reads ALL ECHOS (1).
  • Desktop and phone. Version v4.3.1, new offline cache (tessdesk-v4.3.1). Windows download: TessDesk Setup 4.4.1 (installs widget v4.3.1).

v4.3 Oct 1, 2026

  • Rate status, always visible. Uses your PSO RSEV rates (editable in Settings → Rates). Charging at a high rate: a red (2–7 PM summer weekday peak) or amber (other daytime) pill: "PEAK RATE · charging costs more · off-peak starts 11 PM (in 1h 12m)", with the price now per kWh and the estimated extra cost for this session vs off-peak, a one-tap Stop charging (still asks to confirm) and the Scheduled Charging tip. Charging off-peak: a green pill "OFF-PEAK · 3.0¢/kWh · cheapest rate until 6 AM". Not charging: a small line like "Peak now · off-peak in 1h 12m" or "Off-peak now · ends 6 AM". Superchargers / DC fast charging are skipped. The desktop also shows one Windows notification per high-rate session. Looks the same in Full and COMPACT.
  • Charging-started announcement (desktop): when the car goes from not charging to charging, Alexa says so once per session after one refresh (real kW and amps), including "at the off-peak rate" or "at the peak rate, which costs more". On/off switch in Setup; only after the Alexa disclosure is accepted; only one of your PCs announces it.
  • All Echos: announcements go to every Voice Monkey speaker by default. Setup has a speaker section (All Echos, or pick speakers) and Refresh devices. The nightly reminder still goes to the Living Room Echo.
  • Vertical charge-limit slider next to the battery: drag up and down (or use the mouse wheel / arrow keys on the desktop). The big thumb shows the limit % and the estimated miles, live while you drag. 50–100 %, a green 80 % DAILY mark and an amber 90 % mark. After you let go it asks "Set to X%?" (Confirm / Cancel). It replaces the old horizontal limit handle; the battery bar still shows the limit as a marker. Fits COMPACT too.
  • Announce on Alexa: one push speaks a full status rundown on your Echo (battery and range, limit, charging and kW / amps, time to full, tonight’s and last charge cost, climate and seats, locks, windows, all four tires vs recommended, warnings, the current rate period, and when the data was updated). Under ~45 s, split into two announcements when needed. Asks "Announce full status?" first, then shows a notice. Setup (gear next to it): turn each item on or off, All on / All off, and Preview the exact words without announcing. If the Alexa disclosure isn’t accepted yet, it opens Connected apps instead.
  • Version v4.3, new offline cache (tessdesk-v4.3). The narrower v4.2.1 phone layout is unchanged.

v4.2.1 Oct 1, 2026

  • Phone app about 10 mm narrower so it sits better on a phone: the app is now 38 px narrower than the screen (19 px more margin on each side) and stays centered. On a 390 px-wide phone the cards went from 358 to 320 px wide (max width 480 → 442 px on big screens).
  • COMPACT is narrower too (276 → 238 px on a 390×844 phone), slightly tighter spacing and drawings, and it now fits small phones like 375×667 without scrolling. On 390×844 the compact text is a little bigger than before.
  • On narrow phones the temperature control gets its own row under Vent / Close, long button captions wrap instead of spilling out, and the header brand shrinks a little so "Logged in as …" isn't cut off. No more sideways scrolling on 360 px phones.
  • Phone app only. The desktop widget is unchanged.

v4.2 Oct 1, 2026

  • Charging: Start / Stop charging buttons that show the current state (green = charging; Stop asks first), and a CHARGING AMPS slider under the limit slider. Drag, let go, confirm. It is clamped to what your charger allows (min 5 A).
  • Climate: new HEAT (climate on at a warm 82 °F), max DEFROST, and Cabin Overheat Protection (Off → On → Fan only, if your car supports it). Each shows its state.
  • Heated seats, a new card drawn like the tires: tap a seat to cycle Off → 1 → 2 → 3 (sent a moment after your last tap), plus steering-wheel heat.
  • Windows: the button that matches the car turns green (Vent when any window is open, Close when they're all closed).
  • Money rows (tonight / 7 / 30 days) moved up under the big number and made compact. Bigger, bolder section titles and a larger tires "as of" time.
  • Alexa announcements (optional) through Voice Monkey: an ALEXA switch in the header announces each control result on your Echo after it finishes, including failures. Settings → Connected apps holds Tessie, Voice Monkey (token, speaker, Check, Send test) and Alexa setup links, with a disclosure you must accept.
  • Scheduled announcements of charging cost, tire pressure and vehicle status, each with its own on/off, several times and chosen days. The desktop runs them with Windows scheduled tasks (cached data, the car isn't woken). The phone shows the same settings but can't run on a schedule.
  • Desktop: the window stays 364 wide at the same spot. The lower sections scroll inside the widget so it fits your screen.
  • Faster live updates: TessDesk now refreshes about every 10 seconds while the car is charging, driving or has climate on, every 15 seconds while the app is open (desktop: focused) and the car is awake, 60 seconds when the desktop widget is in the background, and backs off to 2–5 minutes while the car sleeps. It only reads Tessie's cached data, so it never wakes the car. A small "● Updated 6s ago" shows how fresh the data is.
  • FULL / COMPACT switch in the header: Compact keeps every section and control but uses smaller type, tighter spacing and smaller drawings so everything fits the screen with no scrolling. Your choice is remembered.
  • Tesla controls moved above Tires.
  • Tires: "REC" is now plain words ("Recommended 42 PSI"), and the time is a clear "Updated 2:10 AM · Oct 1" pill.
  • Setup: how reminders reach you, a new link under "Remind me to get air". Desktop: email, text (with honest carrier notes), Alexa announcement, Windows notification and a phone calendar alert, in any combination, plus a Send test button. Phone: calendar alert, text or email (ready to send), or announce on Alexa now. Tessie's API can't notify the Tessie app or show a message on the car's screen, so for Tessie's own alerts turn on Tessie app → Notifications → Low tire pressure.

v4.1 Oct 1, 2026

  • Tesla controls, a new card under Tires: Lock/Unlock (shows the current state), Vent / Close windows, Climate (A/C) on/off, and set temperature with − / + buttons (°F or °C, whichever your car uses). Shows a spinner while sending, then OK or the error.
  • Safety prompts: Unlock, Vent windows and charge-limit changes ask "Are you sure?" first.
  • Charge-limit slider: drag the white LIMIT handle on the battery bar. The new % shows large while you drag. Let go, confirm ("Set charge limit to 85%?"), and it's sent to the car. The car's allowed range (usually 50–100%) is enforced.
  • Battery card redesign: current % in large type with range in miles (rated or ideal, matching the car's display), a 0–100% bar with the moving ball, and larger FROM / LIMIT labels with miles above the %.
  • Power tile reads "CHARGING AT 4 kW" with volts and amps under it, or "Not charging" plus the charge state.
  • Tire colors: green = good, yellow + LOW/HIGH = a little out (the car's soft TPMS warning, or 5–10% from the recommended pressure), red + LOW/HIGH = really out (the car's hard TPMS warning, or more than 10% off). Red tires flash. Thresholds are in config.json on the desktop. The header shows the recommended PSI.
  • Remind me to get air, a new button under the tires: pick 1, 2, 4, or 8 hours (or your own number) and get a reminder listing the low tire(s) and their PSI. Desktop: Windows Task Scheduler sends it by email and/or text through your own email account (Gmail + App Password by default), even if TessDesk is closed. The password is stored with Windows DPAPI. Setup has a "Send test reminder" button. Phone: add a calendar event with an alert (.ics or Google Calendar), or open an email draft.
  • Text reminders use carrier email-to-text gateways, and Setup says honestly which still work: AT&T stopped in June 2025, T-Mobile is unreliable, Verizon works until Mar 2027. Email is the reliable choice.
  • Always the Tessie look: the TESSIE LOOK switch is gone. Green while charging, red-tinted when not.
  • Notice & permissions: Setup (and the phone's first-run screen) now explains what TessDesk does with your token and asks for I agree, plus separate permissions: read vehicle data (required), send commands (optional; off = controls disabled), and reminders (optional). Your choice is saved with a timestamp. Existing installs see the notice once and connect to Tessie only after you agree.
  • About / Privacy link in the footer, a new privacy & disclosures page, and PRIVACY.md in the download.
  • Getting started / Don't have these yet? panel with links to sign up for Tessie, get your API token, create a Gmail account, and make a Gmail App Password (in Setup's token step, About / Privacy, and the phone's setup).
  • The desktop token is now stored encrypted with Windows DPAPI (tessie.token.dpapi) when you run Setup.
  • Desktop window is taller (364 wide, same spot) so nothing gets clipped. It grows automatically if it ever needs more room.
  • The version in the footer opens this page.
