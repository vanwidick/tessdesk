Every TessDesk version (desktop widget and phone app), newest first.

v4.3.21 Oct 9, 2026 (desktop and phone)

LATEST
  • HEALTH HISTORY moved into the CHARGING card, right under START / STOP and above the charging amps. It works as before: starts closed, remembers open or closed, shows the latest % and the 30-day change, and opens to the list, the summary and Show all. BATTERY HEALTH keeps the big %, kWh, sparkline and tips.
  • Calibration info: days that look like a BMS recalibration (a jump of 1.5 points or more, or a one-day spike that goes back the next day) get a small CAL tag. These are changes in the estimate, not in the battery. A Calibration line shows the last one and how many days ago it was, plus a short tip: now and then charge from about 10-20% to 90-100% and let the car sleep for an hour or more.
  • Battery health history runs fully inside the app with your own Tessie token and car: the first run fetches all the past daily data Tessie has, then it logs once a day, catches up on missed days when you come back, and shows the last data when you are offline. The car is never woken for it.
  • Desktop v4.3.21, Windows download: TessDesk Setup 4.4.21. Phone app v4.3.21: open TessDesk on your phone and tap the refresh banner.

v4.3.19 Oct 8, 2026 (desktop and phone)

  • 30% wider on the desktop (473 px). The battery bar, the amps slider and the buttons use the extra room, and the SKIP CONFIRM labels (including Announce) show in full. A saved window position keeps its right edge and grows to the new width.

Under the LEAVING SOON button, Windows after and Unlock after set the minutes (0-30, default 3) with - / + buttons, the mouse wheel or Up / Down, and TessDesk remembers them. Keep the page open during the countdown: a phone page cannot run while the screen is locked, so the countdown uses real clock times and catches up when you come back (a step that came due runs then and says how late; more than 30 minutes late it is not run).

v4.3.10 Oct 3, 2026

  • TOTALS pop-up (desktop and phone). A $ TOTALS button under Last 30 days opens a list with running totals for this week (Monday to Sunday), this month and this year: kWh, cost, number of charging nights and the average cents per kWh. Below it is a month-by-month list for the year (kWh, cost, nights), with the current month marked “so far”. Tap a month to see each night.
  • What you paid. Home totals count only charging at home (3515 W 41st Pl) at the all-in PSO rate, priced by the minute (energy + fuel charge: 6.23¢/kWh from 11 PM to 6 AM, the day rate at other hours). Supercharger charging is listed separately with the amount Tessie reports as paid, and both are added up in a grand total.
  • Fast to open. The charge history is saved on your device, so the list opens right away. It refreshes this month in the background, and the Refresh button loads the whole year again with a count (“Loading charge history 4 / 10 months”) and a Stop button. Close with X or Esc.
  • Desktop and phone now match. Both use one shared rule for Last 7 days, Last 30 days and the totals. Before, the phone counted only the part of a charge that fell inside the 7 or 30 days, while the desktop counted the whole charge, so a charge that crossed the cut-off made them differ by a few cents. Now both count every charge that started in the period, and a Supercharger charge counts what was paid.

v4.3.3 Oct 3, 2026

  • OPEN TRUNK. A new button opens the rear trunk after an “Are you sure?” pop-up. If the trunk is already open it reads TRUNK OPEN and asks before closing it. There is no frunk button.
  • SENTRY MODE on/off. One button shows the car’s current Sentry state (green outline and text when on) and asks to confirm before turning it on or off.
  • DRIVES.
