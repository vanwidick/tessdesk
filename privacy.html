About, privacy & disclosures

TessDesk v4.1 · desktop widget and phone app · Design by Van

Unofficial not affiliated

TessDesk is an unofficial app. It is not made by, affiliated with, or endorsed by Tesla, Inc. or Tessie.

What it reads & does

  • It uses your Tessie API token to read vehicle data: location, battery and range, charging, tire pressures and warnings, lock state, windows, and climate.
  • It sends commands only when you press a control: lock/unlock, vent/close windows, climate on/off, cabin temperature, charge limit. Unlock, vent, and charge-limit changes ask you to confirm.
  • Regular updates use Tessie's cached state and don't wake the car. Commands can wake the car and use a little battery.
  • Use the controls only when it's safe and legal (not while driving).

Where your data lives

  • Phone: your token and settings stay in this browser's local storage, on this phone only.
  • Desktop: stored only on your PC in the TessDesk folder. The token and email App Password are encrypted with Windows DPAPI (readable only by your Windows account).
  • The token is sent only to api.tessie.com. There's no TessDesk server, no analytics, and no tracking. Nothing is sent to the TessDesk author.

Reminders

  • Desktop: "Remind me to get air" schedules a one-time Windows Task Scheduler task that sends through your own email account (SMTP), as email and/or a text through your carrier's email-to-SMS gateway. It works while you're signed in to Windows.
  • Carriers are shutting these gateways down. AT&T stopped in June 2025, T-Mobile is unreliable, and Verizon ends by Mar 31, 2027. Email is the reliable choice.
  • Phone: a calendar event with an alert (.ics or Google Calendar) or an email draft you send yourself. The web app can't send anything in the background.

Costs, warranty, terms

  • Charging costs are estimates based on the rates and efficiency you enter, not your utility bill.
  • Provided as is, with no warranty. You use TessDesk at your own risk.
  • Tessie's Terms of Service apply to your Tessie account and API token.

Permissions

  • Read vehicle data from Tessie (required)
  • Send vehicle commands (optional; off = the controls and charge-limit slider are disabled)
  • Reminders by email/text (optional)

Your choice is saved with a timestamp (desktop: config.json; phone: this browser). Change it in Settings → Permissions (phone) or About / Privacy (desktop).

Revoke or uninstall

  • Revoke access: delete the API token in the Tessie app (Settings → API) or at dash.tessie.com/settings/api.
  • Desktop: double-click "Uninstall TessDesk.cmd" in the TessDesk folder (%LOCALAPPDATA%\TessDesk). It also removes reminder tasks, shortcuts, the token, and the App Password.
  • Phone: Settings → Log out and erase data, then remove the home-screen icon.
